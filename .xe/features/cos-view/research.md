## Findings (what wins)

| # | Finding |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | One finite screen: next meeting, 3 messages, 3 actions, 1 task. No feed. |
| 2 | Every item carries its summary and a ready answer; one tap to act. |
| 3 | Defer/delegate on every item; never just "dismiss". |
| 4 | Suggest the plan, do not impose it (Morgen over Motion). |
| 5 | A "next N minutes" frame answers "what now" better than a full-day plan. |
